# Ryobi Garage Door Opener (GDO) Integration

Home Assistant custom component for the **Ryobi Ultra-Quiet Garage Door Opener (GD200 / GD201 / GD125)** and its modular accessories.

## Features:
- 🚪 **Cover**: Open and close your garage door with real-time status updates via WebSocket push.
- 💡 **Light**: Control the integrated overhead LED light.
- 🔋 **Battery & Sensors**: Real-time battery charge level, Wi-Fi signal strength, motion detection, and safety beam status.
- 🔌 **Modular Accessories**: Support for modular plug-ins including Park Assist Laser, Inflator, Bluetooth Speaker, and Fan.
- ⚡ **WebSocket Push**: Near instantaneous feedback on all door state changes and sensor activations.

## Setup
1. Search for `Ryobi Garage Door Opener` in **Settings** > **Devices & Services** > **Add Integration**.
2. Enter your Ryobi credentials and choose your garage opener.
